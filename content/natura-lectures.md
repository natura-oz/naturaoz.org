Natura o.z. aims at identification of rising stars in biomedical sciences and supporting
their oral presentations at international scientific meetings. Since 2007, in close
collaboration with the Yeast Commission of the Czechoslovak Society for Microbiology,
we organized the series of invited ‘Natura lectures’ presented at the Annual Conference
on Yeasts organized in Smolenice (Slovakia).
