## By local transportation (bus or tram)

Bus: **28,29,32**

Tram: **1,4,5,9,12**

Bus/Tram stop **“Botanická (záhrada)”**

## From the Bus terminal “Autobusová stanica-Mlynské nivy”

Take the bus no.**70** at “Autobusová stanica-Mlynské Nivy” to “Nový most” (the
terminal is under the bridge) then take the bus no. **28** or **29** or the tram
no. **4** or **12** and stop at “Botanická (záhrada)”.

## From the Railway station “Bratislava -Hlavná stanica”

Take the bus no. **32** at “Hlavná stanica” and stop at “Botanická (záhrada)”

## By car

### From Vienna/Budapest

Enter Bratislava using **E65/D2**.

After crossing the bridge over Danube take the first exit to **Mlynská dolina**,
continute straight ahead (do not turn left) for about 500 m. Turn left (the bridge
over the highway), after the bridge turn again left and then the first right (up
the hill). Continue for about 500 m (do not take the first right), at the end of
the road take right and again right (the faculty (look for a big sign "**Prírodovedecká
fakulta UK**" on top of the building) will be on your left side).

### From Brno

Enter Bratislava using **E65/D2**.

After passing the tunnel, keep in the left lane. Take exit toward
**Mlynská dolina/Karlova ves/Devín** then keep right. After crossing the lights,
take the first right (up the hill). Continue for about 500 m (do not take the first
right), at the end of the road take right and again right (the faculty (look for
a big sign "**Prírodovedecká fakulta UK**" on top of the building) will be on
your left side).
